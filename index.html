Gap 1: You pay to reach Cork, but your profile doesn’t show there on Google Maps

Impact: HighEffort: Medium

The evidence (your data)

  • Google’s Ads Transparency Centre lists 65 ads from the verified advertiser “DARREN DRUMM” (15 image, 39 text, 11 video), last shown 10 Oct 2026. One description reads “Dream kitchen? We design & install bespoke fitted kitchens in Cork. Get a free quote!”, and ads land on drummkitchens.ie and /fitted-kitchens-cork/.
  • Your Meta ads also target Cork, alongside five other towns (see gap 11).
  • On Google Maps you’re not in the top 20 for any of the 4 searches from Cork city or Blarney (0 of 8). Around Mallow you’re #1 or #2 for all 4 searches, for free.
  • On ordinary Google web search from Cork, your website is #6 for “fitted kitchens Cork” and “kitchen showroom Cork”, so the site already does better there than the profile.

How competitors compare

  • Sliding Door Wardrobes Does betterNo Google or Meta ads, yet it’s the only one of the four on Maps from Cork city: #1 for “fitted wardrobes” there, and #4 from Blarney. It’s based in Togher, Cork, with 149 reviews and 9 categories.
  • Woodcraft Kitchens Same as youNo ads. Not in the top 20 from Cork city or Blarney either.
  • Crafted Fitted Furniture Same as youNo Google ads. Not in the top 20 from Cork city or Blarney either.

Action

  1. In your Google Ads account, check which areas and keywords each campaign targets and what it costs. The public library doesn’t show this.
  2. If any budget goes on Mallow-area searches where you’re already #1 or #2 on Maps for free, move it towards Cork city.
  3. Treat the ads as a bridge while the profile builds a presence in Cork: the category (gap 3), reviews from Cork customers (gap 4) and Cork area pages (gap 5).

Method and caveats

  • When and how: data from 10 Oct 2026. Google Maps rankings (28 searches), Google web search results (6 searches, Cork location, desktop) and your full listing came from Apify. Competitor listings were checked by hand on Google Maps while signed out, and all four websites were crawled lightly (up to 30 pages each, plus each site's sitemap). Website findings about your own site (the broken Fitted Kitchens link, the Newmarket text) come from the v1 audit. On 11 Oct 2026 we re-checked the broken link and the old domain, compared each site's home and contact pages with its Google listing, and collected the social links each site shows.
  • Some data points need a further check. They're marked Request data check and haven't been estimated.
  • Ad libraries: Google Ads Transparency Centre and Meta Ad Library, both checked signed out on 10 Oct 2026 with the region set to Ireland, searching each business by name. Google's library shows ads, formats, dates and landing pages, but not targeting, keywords or spend. Meta's shows ads, dates, platforms and, for EU ads, the targeted locations, ages, genders and an estimated reach.
  • No spend data: neither library shows how much any business spends, so nothing here says or implies a budget. Check your own spend in your Google Ads and Meta accounts.
  • Maps results depend on where the searcher is. Each search was run from the centre of one town, zoomed to about 13 km. Someone searching from a different street, or on a phone with location turned on, can see a different order.
  • Proxies aren't traffic. Rankings, review counts, photos, ad counts and social figures are public signals. They usually move with demand, but they aren't calls, visits or sales.
  • Signed-out view: Google currently shows a “limited view” of Maps when you're signed out. It hides review lists, so competitors' review dates, reply rates and star breakdowns couldn't be read by hand. Rating, categories, photos, attributes, phone, website and hours were visible.
  • Website measures: “service pages” are pages about a product or service (not projects, blog, legal or contact pages), counted from each sitemap. “Town / location pages” have a place name in the address. “LocalBusiness schema” means LocalBusiness or a subtype in the page's structured data.
  • Impact and effort tags are our judgement, based on what each gap affects (being found, or what people see after clicking) and how much work the fix is.
  • Small samples: one snapshot on one day. Rankings move from day to day, so treat differences of one or two places as noise.
